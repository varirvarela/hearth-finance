Design Recommendation — Budget Tiles

  • Group sections with tile grids replace the current full-width row list. Each group has a header showing total spent vs budget, then a 4-column grid (desktop) / 2-column (mobile) of category tiles.
  • Left accent bar color encodes status at a glance: green = under pace, amber = over pace (approaching limit), red = over budget.
  • Status dot (top-right of tile) gives a second color signal without needing to read numbers. Over-budget dot pulses with a red glow.
  • Mini bar + percentage at the tile bottom is the most compact way to show progress — no full-width bar needed per row.
  • Pace tick on the overall bar shows today's elapsed month proportion so you can instantly see if you're ahead or behind on total spending.
  • Clicking a tile could navigate to Transactions pre-filtered to that category + current month (same drill-down that exists from Dashboard today).
  • Annual categories (car service, insurance) show a note "annual — not yet" rather than forcing a monthly % comparison that would always look alarming.
  • Tile grid vs current list: shows ~3× more categories without scrolling on desktop; groups collapse the hierarchy so context is maintained.
